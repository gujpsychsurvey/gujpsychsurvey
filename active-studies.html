GujPsychSurvey · Studies currently recruiting participants

This page lists research studies in Gujarat that are open for participation right now. Each study is run by its own researchers on their own survey platform. GujPsychSurvey only lists the study and sends you to the researcher's link; it does not host the questionnaire or collect your responses.

Every study listed here has been submitted with Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) approval details, which have been checked administratively. Please read each entry carefully and decide for yourself. Taking part is entirely voluntary.

Before you click: Read the consent information on the researcher's survey. Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, Aadhaar, PAN or bank details with any study.

How to read the table

  • Study title and short description: What the study is about, in one or two lines.
  • Principal investigator & PI details: Who is responsible for the study, and where they work.
  • Survey language: Gujarati, English, Hindi, or a combination.
  • Who can take part: The inclusion and exclusion criteria. Please take part only if you are eligible.
  • Time needed: The estimated time to complete the study.
  • IEC details: The ethics committee that approved the study and its approval number.
  • Participate: Opens the researcher's own survey in a new tab. The link is shown only while recruitment is open.
