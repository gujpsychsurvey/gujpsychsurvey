Understanding GujPsychSurvey

A transparent, non-custodial bridge connecting researchers with participants.

What is GujPsychSurvey?

A free, open directory of research studies that need participants. You browse the list, read who is eligible, and follow a link to the researcher's own survey.

Who is it for?

People in Gujarat who would like to contribute to research, and researchers who need participants beyond their own institutional networks.

How are studies listed?

Researchers submit an application with their ethics committee approval. We check the researcher and the approval before a study is published.

What we do not do

No login, no registration, no analytics or tracking tools, and no collection of your personal details. We do not host questionnaires or store responses.

Disclaimer

GujPsychSurvey is a voluntarily maintained, independent directory. Each listed study is run by its own researchers, who are solely responsible for it, and a listing is not an endorsement. Participation is voluntary. This site gives no medical advice.
