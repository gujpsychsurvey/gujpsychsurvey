GujPsychSurvey · Mental Health Support

If you or someone you know needs mental health support, this page lists helplines, government hospitals, and other resources available in Gujarat and India. Taking part in a research study is not a substitute for care.

In an emergency

If someone is in immediate danger, call 112 (national emergency number), call 108 for an ambulance, or go directly to the nearest government hospital casualty department.

National Helplines

Tele-MANAS

Government of India · Ministry of Health and Family Welfare · Free · 24x7

Free, 24×7 mental health support helpline available across India. Provides counselling, psychological first aid, and referral services. Gujarati-speaking counsellors are available. No registration required.

Call 14416

iCall

TISS Mumbai · Monday to Saturday, 8 AM – 10 PM

Provides individual counselling and support via phone and email. Trained psychologists and counsellors available.

Call 9152987821

Vandrevala Foundation

Free · 24x7 · Multilingual including Gujarati

Free mental health support helpline available in multiple Indian languages including Gujarati, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Call 1860-2662-345 Call 1800-2333-330

Other Services & Bodies

Government Medical College Psychiatry Departments

Available across Gujarat

Outpatient and inpatient psychiatric services are available at the Department of Psychiatry in all government medical colleges across Gujarat, including Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, Rajkot, Jamnagar, Bhavnagar, Patan, Valsad, Gandhinagar, and others. Contact your nearest government medical college hospital for appointments and services.

District Mental Health Programme (DMHP)

National Mental Health Programme - Available across Gujarat districts

Government community mental health services available at district hospitals across Gujarat. Provides outpatient psychiatric consultations, medication, and counselling through district hospitals and primary health centres. Contact your nearest district hospital for details.

Indian Psychiatric Society - Gujarat State Branch (IPS-GSB)

Founded 1970 - Oldest and largest mental health professional body in Gujarat

The IPS-GSB is the oldest and largest organisation of mental health professionals in Gujarat, working to advance the field of mental health across Gujarat and India.

Visit ipsgsb.org

Indian Psychiatric Society (IPS)

Founded 1947 - National organisation of psychiatrists in India

The Indian Psychiatric Society is the national organisation of psychiatrists in India, founded in 1947 during the Indian Science Congress in New Delhi. It works to promote mental health education, research, and clinical practice across the country.

Visit indianpsychiatricsociety.org

Concerns about a study

If a listed study seems unsafe, misleading, does not match its description, or asks for sensitive details, please write to us with the study name. We will review it and may suspend the listing. For concerns about how a study is run, you can also contact the ethics committee named on its listing.
